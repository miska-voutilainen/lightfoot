Run `lightfoot why` in the shell for the most recent session, or `lightfoot why <session id prefix>` for another one.

Explain it to the user in plain words: what was condensed, roughly how much it saved, and where the full text of anything condensed is kept. Don't add numbers that aren't in the output.
